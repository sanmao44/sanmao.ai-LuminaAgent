# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Graphic designers, retouchers and film/post-production professionals. Their primary jobs are Agent conversation, image generation and video generation.

## Product Purpose

SANMAO.AI is a multi-model AI creative studio that turns a brief into editable image, video and Canvas work.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve existing Agent, image, video, task recovery and Super Canvas capabilities.
- PC is the primary environment. Prioritize wide desktop workspaces and keyboard interaction; mobile is a fallback.
- Super Canvas is a product highlight and must remain visually prominent.
- Keep the locked Modular Next Monolith boundaries and existing domain owners.

## Brand Commitments

The name SANMAO.AI remains. The requested direction is modern, intelligent, convenient, premium and aligned with current 2026 product design practice.

## Product Principles

- Make the next creative action obvious.
- Keep AI state visible without turning every state into a colored card.
- Let the work occupy the visual field; chrome recedes.
- Preserve human review and editability.
